# Literature screen (how the observations were found)

*Plain-language note: the search steps behind `observations.csv`, so anyone can repeat or extend them.*

- **Engine:** OpenAlex API (https://api.openalex.org/works?search=...), 2026-10-13, 20 to 25 results per query, about 360 unique works in total. This was supplemented by the reference lists of two open-access articles: Zheng et al., *Rare Metals* 38 (2019) 151 (doi:10.1007/s12598-017-0879-8), and Yan et al., *Rare Metals* 37 (2018) 232 (doi:10.1007/s12598-018-1004-3).
- **Queries:** TbCu7 Sm Fe melt-spun · TbCu7 Sm-Fe mechanical alloying · SmFe9 TbCu7 · (Sm,Zr)Fe TbCu7 rapidly quenched · Sm-Fe-{Ti, V, Nb, Si, Ga, Al, Cr, Cu} melt-spun · Sm-Fe-Mo melt-spun ThMn12 · Sm-Fe-Mn rapidly quenched · Sm Fe Co TbCu7 melt-spun · Sm Fe Hf TbCu7 · SmFe12 ThMn12 rapid quenching metastable · Sm3(Fe,Ti)29 melt spun · Sm2Fe17 mechanical alloying metastable · Sm Fe Zr ThMn12 melt-spun · Sm2Fe17-xMx melt spun / mechanical alloying (M = Al, Ga, Si) · SmFe10M2 melt-spun / mechanically alloyed (M = Mo, Cr, V) · Sm2(Fe,Mn)17 mechanically alloyed · SmFe11Ti mechanical alloying · Sm(Fe,Ti)7 TbCu7 · SmFe7 Ti TbCu7 melt spun · Sm(Fe,V)12 melt spun · Sm-Fe-Zr TbCu7 mechanical alloying · Sm2(Fe,Si)17 mechanical alloying · Sm(Fe,Co)7 melt spun · Sm-Fe-Ti mechanical alloying ThMn12 · SmFe12 melt spun TbCu7 Ti · Sm(Fe,Si)12 ThMn12 melt-spun · Sm-Fe-Cr ThMn12 rapidly quenched · Sm-Fe-Al ThMn12 melt-spun · Sm Fe Mn ThMn12 melt spun · Sm-Fe-Co mechanical alloying TbCu7 · SmFe9 Ti TbCu7 · SmFe12 Nb melt-spun · Sm Fe Ga TbCu7 melt-spun nitride.
- **Inclusion:** Sm-Fe or Sm-Fe-M (M in the core set) made by melt spinning, rapid quenching or mechanical alloying/milling (plus anneal), with the phase identity stated in the open abstract or the title.
- **Excluded from scoring (kept as context, `scored = no`):** quinary alloys, other routes (reduction-diffusion), amorphous-only results, and results whose phase structure was not given.
- **Screened but not used (phase not in open metadata or out of scope):** Saito, JMMM 2021 (Sm,Zr)Fe10 (doi:10.1016/j.jmmm.2021.168573); Saito, JMMM 2022 Sm(Fe,Co)10 + Zr/Ti (doi:10.1016/j.jmmm.2022.170233); Xiao et al., JMMM 1995 SmFeTi (doi:10.1016/0304-8853(94)01251-2); Schultz et al., MSE A 1991 (doi:10.1016/0921-5093(91)90034-k); Ding & Rosenberg, JLCM 1990 (doi:10.1016/0022-5088(90)90012-9); Lu et al., JMMM 2018 (doi:10.1016/j.jmmm.2018.01.071); Liu et al., JMMM 1994 (doi:10.1016/0304-8853(94)90285-2); Wang et al., JAP 1990 Sm-Fe-Ti-V (quaternary); many Sm-Co-based melt-spun magnets (Fe minor, outside the Sm-Fe-M screen); Sm-Fe-M-C and Sm-Fe-M-N interstitial compounds (C or N changes the phase stability).
- **No qualifying observation found for:** M = Cr, Mn and Hf (Hf appears only in a reduction-diffusion study). For Al and Cu, only the Singleton 1989 annealed ThMn12 alloys qualify.
